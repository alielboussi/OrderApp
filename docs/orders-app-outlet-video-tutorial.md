# Afterten Orders — Outlet Video Tutorial (Production Pack)

Use this document to **record a screen-capture tutorial** for outlet staff. It covers everything on the **outlet side** of the app only (not supervisor tools).

**Estimated finished video length:** 14–18 minutes  
**Recommended format:** 1080p screen recording on a phone or tablet, with voice-over in English (or read the script in another language).

---

## Before you record

### What you need

- [ ] A test outlet account (email + password)
- [ ] The Afterten Orders app installed (APK or Expo build — not supervisor-only login)
- [ ] At least one order in each status for demo, **or** record in two sessions:
  - Session A: place a new order + submit damage report
  - Session B: receive a loaded order (ask supervisor to dispatch a test order first)
- [ ] Screen recorder (built-in Android recorder, or OBS if mirroring to PC)
- [ ] Quiet room; phone on Do Not Disturb

### Demo data checklist

| Scene | You need |
|-------|----------|
| Login + home | Outlet credentials |
| Create order | 2–3 products (one with variants if possible) |
| Pending — placed | Order just placed, status **Pending Approval** |
| Pending — accepted | Order supervisor already accepted |
| Pending — loaded | Order supervisor already dispatched (**Loaded & On Route**) |
| Completed | At least one completed order for PDF demo |
| Damages — submit | Camera permission; sample damaged products |
| Damages — pending | One damage report awaiting or on route |

### Recording tips

1. **Portrait mode** — the app is designed for phone portrait.
2. **Slow taps** — pause half a second on each button so viewers can follow.
3. **Hide sensitive data** — use a demo outlet name or blur if needed.
4. **One finger for signatures** — draw slowly in one continuous stroke (the app rejects short or broken signatures).
5. **Chapter markers** — add YouTube chapters or split into short clips using the sections below.

## Full narration script

Read this while recording, or record voice-over separately and sync in an editor.

---

### Chapter 1 — Introduction & login (0:00 – 1:30)

**ON SCREEN:** App icon → open app → login screen

> Welcome to the Afterten Orders app. This app is for **outlet staff** to order stock from the warehouse, track deliveries, sign when stock arrives, and report damaged products.
>
> Each outlet has its own login. Open the app and enter your **email** and **password**, then tap **Sign In**.

**ACTION:** Enter email, enter password, tap **Sign In**.

> If login fails, check your email spelling and password with your manager. Only use the account assigned to your branch.

---

### Chapter 2 — Home screen tour (1:30 – 2:30)

**ON SCREEN:** Outlet home — title “Afterten Orders”, outlet name, four buttons

> After login you see your **home screen**. Your outlet name is shown at the top.
>
> There are four main buttons:
>
> **Create New Order** — place a new stock order to the hub.  
> **Pending Orders** — orders and damage reports still in progress.  
> **Completed Orders** — finished orders you can download as PDF.  
> **Damages** — report products that are damaged.
>
> **Log out** is at the top right when you finish your shift or hand the phone to someone else.

**ACTION:** Slowly highlight each button (tap and back, or point with cursor if mirroring).

---

### Chapter 3 — Create a new order (2:30 – 6:30)

**ON SCREEN:** Create New Order

> Tap **Create New Order** to open the product catalog.
>
> Scroll through products or use **search** at the top to find an item quickly.
>
> For a simple product, use the **minus** and **plus** buttons to set quantity, or tap the number to type a quantity.
>
> Some products are **greyed out** or show **Out of stock** — you cannot add those when warehouse stock is low.

**ACTION:** Search for a product, add qty with + button.

> Products with **variants** — for example different sizes or flavours — open a popup when you tap the product. Choose the variant and set quantity there, then close the popup. You can keep browsing and add more products.

**ACTION:** Tap a variant product, pick variant, set qty, close.

> Some items automatically add **companion products** — for example lids with cups. You will see these on the review screen; you do not need to add them yourself.

**ACTION:** Tap **Proceed** or review button at bottom of create-order flow.

**ON SCREEN:** Order review screen

> On the **review screen**, check the date, time, outlet name, and order number preview.
>
> Review every line: product name, quantity, unit, and price. Check the **grand total** at the bottom.
>
> Enter your **employee name** — use your real name; the app capitalises it correctly.
>
> Tap to open the **signature pad**. Draw your signature in **one continuous stroke** without lifting your finger. If the app says the signature is too short, draw a longer line.
>
> Tap **Place order**.

**ACTION:** Enter name, draw signature, place order.

> Your order is now sent to the warehouse. Status is **Pending Approval** until a supervisor accepts it. You will see it under **Pending Orders**.

**ACTION:** Navigate to Pending Orders briefly to show new order, then return home.

---

### Chapter 4 — Pending orders (6:30 – 9:00)

**ON SCREEN:** Pending Orders list

> Tap **Pending Orders** to see everything still in progress. This list includes **orders** and **damage reports**.
>
> For **orders**, you may see these statuses:
>
> **Pending Approval** — you placed the order; the supervisor has not accepted yet. You can open it to view details but you cannot change quantities.
>
> **Order Updated** — the supervisor changed quantities before accepting. Open the order to see what changed.
>
> **Order Accepted** — the warehouse accepted your order and is preparing it. Wait for dispatch.
>
> **Loaded & On Route** — the stock is on the truck. You can start receiving when it arrives — we cover that next.

**ACTION:** Open a pending-approval order (read-only view), go back. Open an accepted order, go back.

> Damage reports in this list are tagged **Damage report**. They follow their own steps — we cover damages later.

---

### Chapter 5 — Receive delivery — offloader sign (9:00 – 12:00)

**ON SCREEN:** Open order with status **Loaded & On Route**

> When the truck arrives, open the order that shows **Loaded & On Route**.
>
> Tap **Start delivery checklist**.

**ON SCREEN:** Delivery checklist

> Tick each product as it is **offloaded and received** at your outlet. This helps you confirm nothing was missed.
>
> When all lines are ticked, tap **Continue to offloader signature**.

**ON SCREEN:** Offloader signature screen

> Enter the **offloader name** — the person physically receiving the stock.
>
> Draw their **signature** on the pad, again in one continuous stroke.
>
> Tap **Finalize delivery**.

**ACTION:** Complete checklist and signature (use a test order).

> The order is now **Completed**. The warehouse is notified. You can find it under **Completed Orders** and download a PDF with all signatures.

---

### Chapter 6 — Completed orders & PDF (12:00 – 13:30)

**ON SCREEN:** Completed Orders

> Tap **Completed Orders** to see finished orders and damage reports.
>
> Each row shows the order or report number and date.
>
> Tap the **PDF** icon to generate and download the signed document. You can share or print this for your records.

**ACTION:** Tap PDF on one completed order, show download/share sheet briefly.

> Keep completed PDFs for stock records or if you need to query a delivery later.

---

### Chapter 7 — Report damages (13:30 – 16:30)

**ON SCREEN:** Home → Damages

> If products arrive **damaged**, tap **Damages** on the home screen — not Create Order.
>
> Search and add each damaged product and quantity, the same way as ordering.
>
> You **must take a photo** of the damaged products. Tap **Take photo**, allow camera access if asked, and capture a clear picture showing the damage.
>
> Enter **your name** as the person reporting.
>
> Tap **Submit damage report**.

**ACTION:** Add 1–2 lines, take photo, enter name, submit.

> The report goes to the supervisor for approval. It appears in **Pending Orders** with the label **Damage report** and status **Awaiting supervisor approval**.
>
> You cannot complete a damage delivery until the supervisor accepts it and the warehouse dispatches replacement stock.

---

### Chapter 8 — Track & complete damage delivery (16:30 – 18:30)

**ON SCREEN:** Pending Orders → damage report → loaded status

> After approval, your damage report moves through the same delivery steps as a normal order:
>
> **Accepted** — approved, waiting for pickup at warehouse.  
> **Loaded & On Route** — replacement stock is on the truck.
>
> When it arrives, open the damage report from **Pending Orders**, run the **delivery checklist**, then **offloader signature**, and tap **Finalize delivery** — exactly like a normal order.

**ACTION:** If you have a loaded damage report, walk through checklist + sign. If not, stay on pending screen and explain.

> When completed, the damage report appears in **Completed Orders** with a PDF.

---

### Chapter 9 — Quick recap & logout (18:30 – 19:30)

**ON SCREEN:** Home screen

> Quick recap for outlet staff:
>
> **Create New Order** — order stock, sign, send to warehouse.  
> **Pending Orders** — track status until delivery.  
> When status is **Loaded & On Route** — checklist, offloader sign, finalize.  
> **Completed Orders** — download PDF.  
> **Damages** — photo + products, submit; then same delivery sign-off when loaded.
>
> Always **log out** when you are done, especially on a shared device. Tap **Log out** at the top right.
>
> Thank you for using Afterten Orders.

**ACTION:** Tap Log out → end recording.

## Common mistakes to mention in the video

| Mistake | What to say |
|---------|-------------|
| Signature too short | “Draw one long continuous line — do not tap dots.” |
| Trying to edit after placing | “Only the supervisor can change quantities after you submit.” |
| Starting delivery too early | “Wait until status says **Loaded & On Route**.” |
| Damage without photo | “Photo is required — the report will not submit without it.” |
| Using Create Order for damage | “Damages have their own **Damages** button on the home screen.” |
| Forgetting logout | “Always log out on shared phones.” |
